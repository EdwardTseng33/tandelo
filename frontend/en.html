Parents ask

Is this a game, or is it math?

Will my child play more than learn?

There is nothing to do in the world that is unrelated to learning. Three short tasks a day, each under ten minutes, nothing after 10:30 pm. Every achievement counts only delayed no-hint checks, never logins, streaks or time spent.

Does the AI give answers?

It never writes the whole solution. It starts from the step your child wrote and nudges forward: ask, point, lend a similar example, show one step. After the fourth level it stops and hands the question to a teammate or teacher.

My child is behind. Will they drag the team down?

Teams are formed from kids tricked by the same monster, so nobody needs to feel ashamed. Dungeons score the team's solve rate; asking for help costs nothing; teammates never see who is stuck.

Does the US$9 plan include a teacher?

No. It is AI practice only. The weekly live class is only in the 8-week plan; teacher check-ins add weekly voice notes and spot checks, but no class.

What if the small group does not fill?

Four students open a group. Three becomes a one-to-three with adjusted tuition. Two or fewer means a full refund. After week four you can stop or continue.
